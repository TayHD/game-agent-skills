# <Project> decisions log

Status: current. Owner: <owner>. Last updated: <date>.

Every standing decision, one line each, grouped by area. Append only: when a decision changes, add a new line that says what it supersedes and leave the old line in place. Read the area's lines before any design call, brief or change in that area.

Line format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · <who decided> · <the decision, in one sentence> · source: <where it was said>`

Mark a call made without the owner as `lead call, pending owner`. Mark a decision found in older notes but not yet confirmed as `to confirm`.

Example (not a real entry): `- 2026-03-04 · owner · Single-player only; co-op is out of scope for the first release. · source: chat`

Only lines that begin with `- ` are entries; the drift check reads them.
